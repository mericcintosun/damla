# Damla pitch script (voiceover, about 2 minutes 24 seconds)

Plain narration to read over the demo video. No timestamps. Natural, calm pace.

---

Sending crypto to someone who is not already set up is painful. You ask for their address, you make them install a wallet, and then you make them buy a gas token just to receive. Most people give up before the money ever arrives.

Damla fixes that. You send money with a link. The person you send it to needs no wallet, no gas, and no signup. They tap the link, and the money is theirs.

Here is how it works. A one time key is created in your browser. The money is locked to that key on Monad, and the key travels only inside the link. When the other person opens it, a relayer submits the claim and pays the gas for them, so they never need a gas token. And by design, that relayer can send the money to exactly one place, the person you chose. It cannot redirect it, it cannot keep it.

Let me show you. I go to send, I pick an amount, and I add a little note. Notice I never had to connect a wallet. Damla gives me a free instant wallet, so there is nothing to install. I create the link, and here it is, ready to share, with a QR code for sending in person.

Now I open that link as the person receiving. I can see the amount, the note, and the proof that the money is really waiting on chain. I tap claim, no wallet, no gas, and it lands. That is a real transaction on Monad, and I can open it in the explorer right here.

There is also a group drop. One link, and the first few people to open it each claim an equal share. Perfect for tipping a group chat or running a giveaway, without collecting a single address. I fund it, I share it, and people claim their share live.

Everything you saw is real. This proof page reads the contract balances straight from the chain, updating every few seconds. And to help you start, the first users get a small welcome gift of MON, so you can try Monad with nothing in your pocket.

Send money like a drop of water. That is Damla.
